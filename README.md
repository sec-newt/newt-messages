# Newt Messages

A private Android app, part of the Newt family (built from
[newt-android-template](https://github.com/sec-newt/newt-android-template)).
It follows the [house style](NEWT_STYLE.md).

> **A personal project, in early development.**

## Install

Releases are built and signed by GitHub Actions. Install and update with
Obtainium from this repository's Releases page.

## Using it

### Chats

- **Days:** a line saying "Today", "Yesterday", or a date like "Mon, Nov 3" shows where
  each day starts.
- **Messages close together are grouped.** Several messages from one person in a few
  minutes show their name once at the top, and their picture and the time once at the
  bottom.
- **Newest:** scroll back up, and a **Newest** button shows in the corner. It says how
  many new messages came in ("3 new"); tap it to jump back down.
- **Attaching:** tap **+**. What's attached shows as small pictures above the message
  box. Tap the **✕** on one to take just that one off.

### Lists and details

- Numbers that aren't saved as contacts show the usual way: (214) 555-0155.
- Group chats show two of their people's colors, so groups are easy to tell apart.
- The People, Groups and Codes tabs show their unread count beside the icon.
- **Group details:** each person is one short row. Tap it to text, call, change their
  color, add them to contacts, remove them, or block them.

### Pictures, videos and sound

- **Videos** show their first frame with **▶ Play video** and how long they are. Tap to
  watch full screen, with big **Pause**, **Save** and **Close** buttons.
- **Voice messages and other sound** have a **▶ Play** button right in the chat, showing
  how far along it is ("0:04 of 0:12"). They play at media volume.
- **GIFs move.** If Android's animations are off (Settings → Accessibility → Remove
  animations), they stay still.
- **Notifications show the picture** someone sent, not just "Picture".
- **Sending a video:** if it's too big for a picture message, it's made smaller first, on
  the phone; this can take a little while. A video too long to fit at a watchable size
  isn't sent, and the message says about how many seconds would fit.

### Keeping things tidy

- **Archive:** hold a chat (or several), then tap the **archive box**. Archived chats leave
  their tab and are marked read; **Archived (2)** at the bottom of the list shows them. A
  chat comes back by itself when someone writes. Hold one there to unarchive it.
- **Deleting a message:** hold it, then **Delete this message**. It's gone straight away,
  with **Undo** at the bottom for a few seconds. Leaving the chat makes it final.
- **Find in this chat:** the magnifying glass at the top of a chat. Matches are
  highlighted; **Older** and **Newer** step through them ("2 of 5"). It looks through the
  messages showing; tap **Show older messages** to look further back.
- **Sign-in codes:** a **Copy 482913** button sits right on the message. The copy is kept
  private and cleared from the clipboard after 2 minutes.

### Settings

- **On the lock screen:** *Show messages*, *Hide what they say* (just who it's from), or
  *Hide everything*. Saying who's messaging follows it while the phone is locked: with
  *Hide what they say* it says who but not the words; with *Hide everything* it stays quiet.
- **Blocked numbers:** everyone you've blocked, with **Unblock**. It's the same list Newt
  Phone uses.
- **About:** the version you have.
